# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Egyptian couples (and their families) planning engagements, weddings, katb kitab, and sebou3 celebrations. They plan events on WhatsApp, browse on mid-range Android phones, read Arabic first but often share bilingual content, and currently spend 2,000–8,000+ EGP on printed cards. Secondary users: wedding planners, photographers, and venues who refer clients for commission. The buyer's context: emotionally invested, comparing against paper invitations and against free Canva images, deciding in a WhatsApp conversation.

## Product Purpose

D3wa sells personalized invitation websites (own link, countdown, one-tap WhatsApp RSVP, map, AR/EN) as a hand-crafted service, priced at or below paper (1,000–2,500 EGP). The storefront's job: make a couple feel "this is fancier than paper and effortless", and get them to tap one WhatsApp button. Success = WhatsApp order messages sent.

## Brand Personality

Warm, celebratory, couture. Like a high-end Egyptian wedding planner's studio: luxurious but personal, never corporate. Arabic-first elegance (calligraphy, ornament used with restraint), festive without being loud. The word "فرحة" (joy) over "product".

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-generated landing pages: identical card grids, gradient text, hero-metric stats, glassmorphism.
- SaaS template shops (Envato/ThemeForest energy): busy, transactional, discount-badge clutter.
- Western minimal wedding SaaS coldness (pure white, thin gray sans, sterile): D3wa is Egyptian and warm.
- Cheap "دعوة الكترونية" WhatsApp-image vendors: pixelated flourishes, WordArt calligraphy, watermark spam.

## Design Principles

1. **The demo IS the pitch.** Show real invitation pages, not abstract promises; every template card should feel like touching the actual product.
2. **WhatsApp is the checkout.** Every path ends in one prefilled WhatsApp tap; remove any friction before it.
3. **Arabic leads, English matches.** RTL is the primary art direction, not a translation afterthought; Arabic typography gets the same care as Latin.
4. **Craft over volume.** Seven excellent templates presented like a boutique collection, not a marketplace of hundreds.
5. **Phone-first ceremony.** Design for a 375px screen passed around a family WhatsApp group; desktop is the adaptation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Mobile-first; WCAG AA contrast targets; full RTL/LTR mirroring; prefers-reduced-motion honored everywhere; works on low-end Android browsers (no heavy JS frameworks); readable Arabic type sizes (Arabic script needs larger x-height compensation).
